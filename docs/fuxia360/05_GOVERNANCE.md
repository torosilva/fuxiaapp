# Fuxia Digital & Fuxia 360 Governance Charter

**Purpose:** Define who decides, who executes and what the system must enforce. This is operational governance, not a corporate shareholder agreement.

## 1. Roles

### Carolina — CEO / Product & Brand
Owns:
- product and assortment
- brand direction
- physical merchandise reality
- commercial priorities
- product cost/price inputs subject to agreed rules
- initial launch priority/tier decisions
- confirmation of merchandise receipts or delegation of that authority

Carolina should not:
- manually re-enter Woo sales
- manually synchronize stock
- chase NovaMktLab for routine commitments
- diagnose Woo/Meta/tracking/cache issues
- act as the human bridge among product, creative, web, CRM and paid media

### NovaMktLab — Growth Execution Partner
Fuxia contracts NovaMktLab as one accountable boutique partner.

#### Goyo — Demand & Creative DRI
Leads:
- content/demand planning
- paid media
- creative briefs
- shooting/production coordination
- campaign assets
- launch demand readiness
- creative testing and learning

#### Adrián — Ecommerce & Technology DRI / NovaMktLab owner
Leads:
- Woo/Bricks ecommerce implementation
- web stability
- checkout
- web performance
- ecommerce UX/CRO implementation
- technical launch readiness
- delivery/accountability of NovaMktLab service

Photographers, models, UGC creators and production resources are resources coordinated through the creative process; they are not separate governance owners unless explicitly contracted otherwise.

### Mario — Digital Growth Lead / Growth Technology & Intelligence
Owns:
- digital operating system
- metric definitions and source-of-truth map
- Growth Platform / Fuxia 360 architecture
- CRM/customer intelligence
- data/instrumentation
- CRO prioritization and measurement
- growth economics
- War Room operating cadence
- system/process design

Mario should not become the replacement human middleware or default Woo webmaster.

## 2. Decision rights

### Carolina may decide within her domain
- product/design
- assortment
- brand
- product production
- normal product priorities
- retail priorities
- receipt confirmation/delegation

### Mario may decide within approved architecture/budget
- data architecture
- technical instrumentation
- Fuxia 360 domain design
- dashboards
- CRM infrastructure
- automation implementation that does not materially change brand/pricing/customer policy
- experiment measurement

### NovaMktLab may decide operationally within approved strategy/budget
- paid allocation within approved guardrails
- audiences
- creative tests
- campaign optimizations
- web implementation approach
- technical solution to approved CRO problems

## 3. Two-key / explicit approval decisions
Exact monetary thresholds are **TBD** and must not be invented by the coding agent.

Require explicit cross-functional approval for examples such as:
- material discount outside approved range
- material paid-media budget increase
- new material vendor/agency spend
- change of inventory source-of-truth
- destructive data migration
- material checkout/payment change
- customer messaging policy/consent change
- major brand-facing redesign
- manual inventory write-off above approved threshold

## 4. Source-of-truth governance
Target:
- Product identity → Fuxia 360
- Physical inventory → Fuxia 360
- Ecommerce order/payment status → WooCommerce
- Unified customer → Fuxia 360
- Loyalty → Fuxia 360
- Launch status → Fuxia 360
- Creative readiness metadata → Fuxia 360; actual media may live in approved storage
- Paid execution → advertising platform
- Growth normalized metrics/analysis → Fuxia 360

There must not be two independent masters for the same fact without an explicit reconciliation rule.

## 5. Merchandise receiving governance
Carolina is accountable that physical receipt quantities are truthful, but may delegate data entry to an authorized operator.

The system must distinguish:
- who entered a draft
- who confirmed the physical receipt
- when it was confirmed
- which location received it

A confirmed receipt creates inventory movements. It must not merely overwrite a stock field.

## 6. Inventory correction governance
Normal sales/transfers/receipts create movements automatically.

Manual corrections require:
- reason
- actor
- before/after or delta
- approval according to threshold/role policy
- audit history

## 7. Operating cadence
Recommended:
- Monday War Room: scorecard, blockers, max five priorities
- Wednesday Growth Check: campaigns, bugs, experiments, launches
- Friday Close: delivered, learned, blocked

The system should eventually support this cadence; it should not create meetings for their own sake.

## 8. NovaMktLab compensation governance
Current operating concept:
- MXN $30,000/month base fee
- paid media separate
- potential quarterly performance bonus up to one monthly fee
- potential sales kicker only above a materially exceeded target and subject to margin/efficiency/returns/tracking guardrails

Exact targets, percentages and caps remain commercial decisions outside the coding agent unless explicitly approved in writing.

## 9. Principle
**The process should carry coordination; people should carry decisions and accountability.**
